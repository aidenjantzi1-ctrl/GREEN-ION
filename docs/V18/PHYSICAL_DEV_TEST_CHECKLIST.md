# V18 So Long — Beginner Motor-Free Physical Development Test Checklist

**Status:** test *instructions*, not results. No rocket or electronics were physically tested in creating this guide. Do **not** install motors or igniters during any step. Keep a responsible adult/mentor involved with LiPo handling, RF repairs, drilling and flight qualification. The GREEN ION device is recording/recovery only; it never controls staging or deployment.

## Before starting — evidence sheet

Write the date, tester, exact Feather/SD/GPS/LoRa/antenna/battery part numbers, current firmware commit/version, 350 mAh LiPo condition, motor-free rocket configuration, instrument model, ambient conditions and photographs. For each row below record **actual value**, **pass/fail**, **log/photo file**, and **repair/retest date**. A missing value is **NOT TESTED**, not PASS.

Manufacturer-only facts: Estes So Long product lists **46.2 in overall length, 1.21 in / 31 mm outside diameter**, dual streamers, and specified E16/F15 single-/two-stage motor families. Estes instructions show an 8 in payload-section tube but **do not specify usable inside diameter or usable axial length**. The manual's weight estimate differs from the current product listing; **weigh the actual assembled rocket**. See [official product](https://estesrockets.com/products/so-long) and [official manual](https://estesrockets.com/cdn/shop/files/Estes-So-Long-Instructions.pdf?v=1722444368). See also [full hardware plan](HARDWARE_PLAN.md).

## Step-by-step test card

| # | What to do (motors removed) | Record and PASS criterion | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Measure payload inner diameter at front/middle/rear, twice per location at 90°; measure nose/coupler intrusion and actual clear axial length. Measure every PCB with connectors/cables and battery L/W/H/mass. | Record **all numbers in mm** and photos. Cardboard/dummy sled slips in/out without forcing, and nose/coupler seat fully; no interference with recovery path. Until measured: **NOT TESTED**. | Stop sled fabrication, adjust arrangement or choose larger airframe. Never treat 31 mm OD as free ID. |
| 2 | With USB/LiPo disconnected, inspect solder, insulation, radio pad, coax and antenna. Meter for short between 3V and GND; check pinouts against exact breakout. | No visible loose/bare conductors, no unexpected short, all connections match V18 agreed wiring. | Leave power off; repair and repeat with mentor. |
| 3 | Power Feather on USB only; check BMP388 and IMU diagnostics. Let barometer stabilize stationary; rotate IMU slowly through axes. | Record pressure/temperature and 60 s stable baseline; no sensor error, no unexplained jumps or bus dropouts; orientation agrees with hand rotation and gravity. Set numeric calibration drift tolerance with Integration Manager/reference instrument **before declaring pass**. | Recheck I2C GPIO22/20, sensor power, bus addresses, mount, chamber vent and software calibration. |
| 4 | Run firmware Full Home Test/SD diagnostic, then make **three** safe simulated recording cycles and read the BINs on PC. Disconnect Wi-Fi while recording. | Three distinct preserved RKT4 v2 BINs, expected 50 Hz configuration, decodable header/records, no reported SD/write/flush failure or overwrite; Wi-Fi loss does not interrupt SD. Save hashes/logs. Synthetic cycles are **not real flights**. | **NO GO**: inspect SD power, CS33, SPI wires/card socket, firmware diagnostics; rerun. |
| 5 | Check LiPo condition, then measure whole-system current/voltage in startup, Wi-Fi idle, armed, SD logging, GPS acquisition, LoRa TX (only after antenna gate), landed/recovery, sleep and wake. Run a supervised representative endurance soak. | No swelling/damage or brownouts. Log mA, peak current, voltage and runtime. Runtime must be **at least 2× the Integration Manager-approved worst-case mission duration** (pad wait + flight + recovery search) with safe voltage reserve. Until mission duration and voltage threshold are agreed: **NOT TESTED**. | Do not assume 350 mAh is enough; reduce load or select physically compatible healthy cell and repeat. No unattended charging. |
| 6 | Inspect **both** rocket and Uno antennas and matching 915 MHz-region connector/feeds with power off. Run passive radio chip/SPI diagnostic before any TX. | No torn RF pad, damaged cable, wrong-band radiator or loose connector; expected SX127x diagnostic/init on both ends. **Never transmit with a missing/damaged/unknown antenna**. | Keep RF TX disabled; repair/replace radio, antenna or connector and repeat inspection. |
| 7 | With verified antennas on both ends, outdoors or suitable open area test 3 controlled Feather packets to Uno and Windows USB/Chrome or Edge Web Serial. Reconnect USB; repeat at safe increasing distances while logging packets/RSSI/SNR. | Three bench packets received and validated, serial reconnect works, GPS/packet age becomes stale appropriately on loss; record distance and packet-loss statistics. **Field range acceptance must be set against the planned recovery area and coordinated radio rules**; three packets alone do not qualify range. | Check Uno regulated breakout 5V-safe interface, CS D4/RST D2/DIO0 D3, shared frequency/packet schema, RF connector, receiver port and permission. |
| 8 | Take final sled outdoors with GPS patch unobstructed; wait for GPS fix. Move by a known short safe distance on foot while receiving. | Log fix time, satellites/quality if available, plausible coordinates, receiver freshness, last-known location and stale/lost indication. Fix-time/accuracy threshold to be set before qualification. | Reposition GPS away from battery/metal, inspect power and I2C; recovery GPS remains unqualified. |
| 9 | With V18 sleep/wake contract confirmed, test recessed physical button **10 times** from actual sleep/landed state, and while recording test for accidental reset only (do not force sleep mid-recording). | Ten intended wake responses, no uncommanded reset, no SD corruption; record GPIO/firmware behavior. **SW38/GPIO38 is a proposal, not confirmed V18 deep-sleep wake**. | Escalate to Primary/Integration; do not drill button access hole or wire unapproved GPIO. |
| 10 | In the **actual assembled bay**, verify BMP388 has a chamber vented to outside static air, isolated from ejection gases. After volume/port calculation and mentor review, test pressure response against reference with controlled elevation/pressure changes. | Port count/diameter/location recorded; clean deburred holes, unobstructed sensor; repeated reference response with documented lag/error tolerance agreed before PASS. | Do not drill by guess; review chamber volume, port location, sealing and sensor orientation. Treat previous unported altitude as potentially distorted. |
| 11 | Insert/remove secured sled 10 times; gently turn/tap rocket by hand while logging; pack actual streamers/wadding and check free travel. | No movement of PCBs/LiPo, no loose RF/SD connection, no resets/data errors, no trapped streamer/shock cord, nose/coupler/stages fit exactly as manual. | Rework standoffs, nonabrasive battery strap, strain relief or sled; never use tape-only retention. |
| 12 | Weigh completed upper stage, booster, electronics and flight-ready rocket with **each intended approved motor combination** and recovery packed. Measure CG and update full-stack/postseparation simulation. | Record grams, CG from nose tip, simulation file, CP/CG margins in both stages and mentor/RSO review. Compare Estes stock CG bands **single stage 27–32 in, two stage 33–37 in** as guidance, not sole acceptance. | **NO GO** until measured stability, stage coupling/separation, motor retention and recovery are independently accepted by launch safety officer. |

## Sign-off rules

- **Recording qualification:** #2–4 pass on actual integrated electronics; GPS/LoRa cannot gate SD-first recording.
- **Recovery electronics qualification:** #5–9 pass, including a safe actual RF link with undamaged antennas, outdoor GPS and measured runtime.
- **Physical rocket qualification:** #1, #10–12 pass and all dimensions, motor/CG/stability and recovery clearance documented. RSO must inspect real flight configuration.
- **No hardware flight-ready declaration** from CI, code, simulated flights or this checklist alone.
- Do not invent test readings or mark a row PASS without logs/photos. Report failed tests with actual observed values and the next repair action on PR #61 or #60.

**Open integration dependencies:** agreed V18 wake/sleep behavior; approved mission duration and battery reserve; radio frequency and packet contract; numeric sensor/pressure/GPS/RF acceptance thresholds; actual tube/board measurements and physical test evidence. No code or frontend changes are authorized by this document.
